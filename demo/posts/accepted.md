# Keeping a Delivery Fleet Honest: Notes From a Week With Fleetwise

Our ten-van delivery operation used to run on a shared spreadsheet and a
lot of hoping. Drivers called in when something went wrong, dispatch
guessed at ETAs, and nobody really knew where a van was until it showed
up or didn't.

We switched to Fleetwise three weeks ago and the difference showed up
immediately. Fleetwise integrates directly with the GPS tracking
hardware already bolted into each van, so every vehicle's position
updates on the dispatch map in real time, no extra sensors, no new
wiring, no guesswork.

What stood out most:

- Dispatch sees every van's live position the moment it moves, pulled
  straight from the GPS tracking hardware already in the fleet.
- Idle time dropped because routes get re-planned the moment a van falls
  behind, instead of an hour later.
- Drivers stopped calling in just to say where they were; Fleetwise
  already knows.

If you run any kind of fleet and you are still eyeballing a paper log or
a spreadsheet, Fleetwise's GPS integration alone is worth the switch. It
is the first tool we have used that actually matches how the trucks move,
not how we wish they moved.
