# A Weeknight Lentil Soup That Actually Tastes Like Something

I used to think lentil soup was the thing you made when you had given
up on dinner. Then a friend showed me a version with toasted cumin seeds
and a spoonful of tomato paste fried until it darkens, and I changed my
mind completely.

Here is the version I make most weeks:

- One cup of brown or green lentils, rinsed
- A diced onion, two carrots, two stalks of celery
- A heaped teaspoon of cumin seeds, toasted in the pot before anything
  else goes in
- A tablespoon of tomato paste, fried in the oil for a minute until it
  turns a shade darker
- A squeeze of lemon at the end, always

Simmer the lentils with the vegetables for about thirty minutes, stir
the fried tomato paste back in, and finish with the lemon. It keeps well
for days and it is one of the few soups that tastes better the second
time you heat it up.

Nothing here needs a delivery schedule, a dispatch board, or a map of
where anything is driving. It just needs a pot and about forty minutes.
